Offline-First Personal Finance

Track Your Cashflow & Calculate Financial Runway

CashRunway is an offline-first mobile application designed to help individuals and founders track expenses, project runway, and maintain financial clarity with zero ads or tracking.

All financial records stay on your device by default. No data selling, no mandatory accounts, and no third-party analytics trackers.

Optional backup to your private Google Drive app folder. Only the app can access its own backup data, preserving full privacy.

📊 Runway Projections

Understand how many months your money will last based on your real burn rate, recurring expenses, and variable income.
