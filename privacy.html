Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 | Last Updated: September 16, 2026

CashRunway (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Core Philosophy: Offline-First

CashRunway is designed from the ground up as an offline-first tool:

  • Your financial transaction records, runway calculations, and budgets remain stored locally on your device in an SQLite database.
  • We do not operate servers that log your financial transactions.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or telemetry systems.

2. Information We Access and Process

A. Local Device Storage

All transaction amounts, categories, dates, and recurring income/expense records you input are stored locally on your device storage. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the app.

B. Google User Data

If you choose to use the cloud backup feature, CashRunway uses Google Sign-In to connect your Google account:

  • Google Profile Data: We access your basic profile (email address, display name, profile photo, and user ID) to authenticate your session and show your account status in the app.
  • Google Drive AppData Scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): CashRunway requests access strictly to its own dedicated application data folder on your Google Drive to store and restore backup files. CashRunway cannot see, access, modify, or delete any personal documents, spreadsheets, photos, or other files in your Google Drive.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
CashRunway's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use and Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data. We do not use your data for targeted advertising, credit scoring, or commercial exploitation. Google user data is used solely to authenticate your identity and facilitate user-initiated backups to your own Google Drive.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Stored locally until you delete individual records or reset app data in the device settings.
  • Google Backups: Saved in your personal Google Drive account. You can delete backups directly within the app or from your Google Drive storage management.
  • Revoking Access: You can disconnect your Google account inside CashRunway or revoke access anytime via Google Account Permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

CashRunway does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us.
